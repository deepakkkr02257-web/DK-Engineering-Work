About Our Company

Trusted Engineering & Metal Fabrication Specialists in Kurla, Mumbai

DK ENGEERING WORK provides comprehensive fabrication solutions for residential, commercial, and industrial requirements. Based out of Kurla (West), Mumbai, we specialize in high-grade iron, mild steel (MS), stainless steel (SS), aluminium, safety gates, window grills, rolling shutters, lift-related fabrication, metro rail-related metal components, car parking sheds, industrial sheds, and customized metal structures.

Our primary focus is on quality workmanship, durable material selection, customized solutions, dependable delivery, and complete customer satisfaction. We execute all engineering projects strictly according to project specifications, client site measurements, and engineering drawings.
